Questions

Everything you need to know

How much does Vaultline cost?

Nothing upfront. Vaultline takes a 10% platform fee per sale — that's it. You keep 90% of everything you earn. No monthly subscription, no listing fee, no hidden charges.

What kind of content can I sell?

Photos, videos, bundles of both — any file you upload. Behind-the-scenes, exclusive sets, tutorials, presets, audio clips. The minimum price per drop is $5.

Do fans need an account to buy?

Fans can browse without an account. To buy and access their library across devices, they sign in via a magic link — no password needed. One tap to their inbox, done.

Are unlocks really permanent?

Yes. Fans pay once and own the content forever. There are no expiring links, no subscription renewals, no "access removed" surprises. What they unlock is theirs.

How do I get paid?

Payments are processed through Stripe. Earnings go directly to your connected bank account. Payouts typically land within 2–5 business days of a sale.

Is my content secure?

Yes. Files are stored in private, encrypted storage and are never publicly accessible. Download links are signed and expire — they can only be accessed by the fan who purchased.
