FAQ

Questions fans and creators ask first.

A paywall product needs trust fast. These answers keep the experience clear before checkout.

What is Vault'd?

Vault'd is a mobile-first website for selling locked digital drops. Creators publish paid content links, and fans pay once to unlock permanent access.

Do fans need an app?

No. Fans can browse public drops, open shared links, pay, and access their library from the website.

What can creators sell?

Vault'd is built for photos, videos, templates, courses, e-books, podcasts, and hosted content URLs. Content still needs to follow platform policy and the law.

Is access permanent?

Yes. Purchases are one-time unlocks. Once a fan buys a drop, it stays in their library unless a refund, dispute, policy issue, or account issue requires review.

What is public vs unlisted?

Public drops can appear in fan discovery and on a creator storefront. Unlisted drops are only reachable by people who have the direct link.

Can creators control downloads?

Yes. A drop can allow downloads, block downloads, or charge an extra percentage when a buyer wants downloadable access.

What is the minimum price?

The creator flow enforces a $5 minimum so checkout fees do not swallow tiny purchases.

How do payouts work?

Real payouts require a connected payout account, identity and bank requirements, and a successful payment processor setup. The wallet shows readiness and recent operations.
